## Project Overview

*Ọgụ Anọ: War of the Four Market Days* is a proposed 2D, story-driven fighting game inspired by Igbo cosmology, language, values, and the traditional four-day market week: Eke, Orie, Afọ, and Nkwọ.

The story follows Adaeze, daughter of the Earth goddess Ala, as she fights to restore the sacred Ọfọ and the balance of the Four Market Days after the fallen priest-king Eze Efu creates a lawless “Fifth Day.” The concept combines competitive fighting-game combat with a branching narrative and moral choices.

## Core Features

- **The Market Clock:** Eke, Orie, Afọ, and Nkwọ rotate during combat, changing the advantages and combat properties available to fighters.
- **Chi Awakening:** A comeback mechanic triggered when a fighter is close to defeat.
- **Ọfọ Verdict:** After a fight, the winner chooses Ebere (Mercy) or Ikpe (Judgement); choices affect the story and endings.
- **Branching Story:** A nine-chapter campaign with four endings, including a secret ending.
- **Igbo Language Layer:** Igbo dialogue, proverbs, move names, English subtitles, and an in-game glossary are planned.
- **Heritage Codex:** An unlockable guide to the concepts, characters, stages, and symbols represented in the game.
- **Mobile-First Design:** Android is the primary platform, with Windows PC also planned; gamepad support is part of the design.

## Planned Roster

The design document specifies eight playable fighters:

1. **Adaeze** — Grappler / all-rounder; Eke affinity.
2. **Ikenna** — Rushdown; Orie affinity.
3. **Nkiruka** — Zoner; Nkwọ affinity.
4. **Ngozi** — Trickster; Afọ affinity.
5. **Ijele** — Tank; Eke affinity.
6. **Ezinne Idemmili** — Flow fighter; Orie affinity.
7. **Dibịa Okoro** — Setup / support; Nkwọ affinity.
8. **Ekwensu** — Wildcard; Afọ affinity.

The document also describes **Ogbunabali** as a sub-boss and **Eze Efu** as the final boss.

## Production Scope

The proposed contest prototype is scoped to:

- Two playable fighters.
- One combat stage.
- The Market Clock mechanic.
- A basic user interface.
- One story cutscene.
- A gameplay trailer.

The full concept outlines later Alpha, Beta, and Launch milestones.

## Cultural and Research Note

The concept combines real Igbo cultural concepts with original fictional worldbuilding and gameplay systems.

The design document states that the Heritage Codex should distinguish cultural material from original inventions. Cultural and language review is an identified production need.
